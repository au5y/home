Embedded World & C++

C++26 is technically complete — the "Fab Four" land

Herb Sutter ↗

The ISO committee finished C++26 in March and it's heading toward publication. Compile-time reflection is the headline feature — Sutter calls it "the most transformative turning point in the language's history." Alongside it: a hardened standard library adding bounds safety to vector/span/string (Google reports 1,000+ bugs fixed and a 30% segfault reduction), contracts (passed 114–12), and std::execution, a unified async/concurrency model. GCC and Clang already implement large parts of it.
